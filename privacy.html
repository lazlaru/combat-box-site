Privacy Policy

Combat Box · Effective November 24, 2026

Combat Box is a game for Mac created by LD Clinton. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

Information we collect

None. Combat Box does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party services.

Data saved on your Mac

The game saves your tour of duty, your settings and the badges you've earned on your Mac so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your Mac and is not synced by the game. Starting a new tour of duty replaces the saved tour, and deleting the app removes everything.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the game sends your mission scores and earned badges to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards and in your achievements. That is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The game itself receives nothing from it except your Game Center name, which is shown on the Badges screen. If you're not signed in, nothing is sent.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the Combat Box support page.
