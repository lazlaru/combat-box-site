Combat Box Support

Help for Combat Box on Mac

Get help

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub. Include your Mac model (for example MacBook Air M2) and macOS version, and say which mission you were flying and what happened.

How to play

Fly in formation
W A S D or the arrow keys move your bomber. Stay inside the ring around your slot: the box's crossfire protects you there.
Guns
Point the mouse where you want to shoot. The gun covering that direction becomes yours. Hold the mouse button to fire it. Your bomber's guns only fire when you fire them. Click a fighter to make it your target. 1 to 6 lock one gun position, and 0 goes back to automatic choice.
Crew jobs
When something goes wrong (a fire, a wound, a jammed gun, a fuel leak), press E to send the right crewman to the most urgent job, or click the job in the crew panel.
Bomb run, when you lead
Steer with A and D to put the crosshair on the aiming point, then press Space when the aiming point reaches the crosshair. The card at the bottom of the screen walks you through it.
Bomb run, on the wing
On some missions another crew leads. Watch the bomber marked LEAD and press Space the moment its bombs fall. The longer you wait, the further long yours land.
Takeoff
Wait for the green flare, hold W for power, keep on the centerline with A and D, and press Space at 110 mph to lift off. Tab hands the takeoff to the pilots, and the briefing has a switch to always let them do it.
Landing
The pilots land her for you. Space skips it. If the gear was damaged, have it cranked down first, or it's a belly landing in the grass.
Emergencies
J jettisons the bombs to lighten the plane. B twice orders everyone to bail out.
Everything else
Esc or P pauses, and M mutes. Music, crew voices and difficulty are on the briefing screen. Music and crew voices are also in the pause menu.

Questions

What's the difference between Easy, Medium and Hard?
On Easy nobody dies and the crew sorts most problems out on its own. On Medium, wounded men die if nobody reaches them in time. On Hard, hits can kill outright, the crew waits for your orders, and when the bomber is lost you must order the bail-out yourself. Harder settings also score more.
Is my tour saved?
Yes, after every mission. Continue tour on the title screen picks up at the next mission. If you quit in the middle of a mission, that mission starts over.
How do I start over?
Choose New tour of duty on the title screen. It replaces the tour in progress.
What is a quick mission?
One of the twenty-five tour missions picked at random, flown on its own. Its score can go on the Game Center leaderboard.
How do badges and leaderboards work?
Sign in to Game Center in System Settings to post scores and badges. Badges you earn while signed out are kept, and sent the next time you're signed in. The Badges screen on the title shows every badge and how to earn it.
Can I turn the music off?
Yes. Use the Music switch on the title screen, the briefing or the pause menu.
Can I play in full screen?
Yes. Choose View › Enter Full Screen, or press Control Command F.
Is it historically accurate?
The twenty-five targets and dates are real Eighth Air Force missions. The crews, bombers and the base are invented, and the game simplifies a great deal.
Does the game collect my data?
No. See the privacy policy.
