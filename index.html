Combat Box

A game for Mac by LD Clinton

You're the pilot of a B-17 Flying Fortress in a six-plane box of the Eighth Air Force. Over twenty-five real missions, from Lorient in May 1943 to Berlin in February 1945, hold your place in the formation, man the guns and bring your crew home.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

  • Fly the bomb run yourself as lead ship, or fly on the lead's wing and drop when his bombs fall.
  • Aim any of six gun positions against Fw 190s, Bf 109s, Ju 88 gunships, rockets and jets.
  • Send your crew to fight engine fires, crank down jammed gear, patch flak holes and look after the wounded.
  • Take off and land at your English airfield. With no hydraulics, it's a belly landing in the grass.
  • Your crew earns experience and medals; replacements arrive when men are lost. Shot down over enemy territory, some may get away along the escape lines and come back.
  • Your bomber wears every patch and replaced panel, and life goes on at the base between missions: passes to London, mail call, a dog that adopts the crew.
  • Let your gunners fire on their own, and take over whichever gun you aim. Green gunners miss more; veterans hit more.
  • An original score, a strike photo with your bombs circled, a map of where the group lost its bombers, and Game Center badges and leaderboards.
  • Play with the keyboard and mouse or a game controller. Quit in the middle of a mission and pick it up again right where you left off.
